ITR Self-Identification Guide

Income Tax Return Filing Framework

Select your structural income streams below to quickly identify and file the correct ITR pathway for your personal or corporate tax profile.

Individual & Professional Filings

ITR-1 (Sahaj)

Salaried Individuals

For salaried professionals earning up to ₹50 Lakhs with standard salary income, one house property, and simple interest/other sources.

  • Single salary income stream
  • Standard deductions & HRA
File ITR-1
ITR-2

Capital Gains & Foreign Assets

For individuals and HUFs with income from capital gains (mutual funds, stocks), multiple residential properties, or foreign investments and assets.

  • Stock market capital gains
  • Foreign bank accounts / stocks
File ITR-2
ITR-3

Business & Freelancers

For business owners, corporate partners, active freelancers, and startup founders with income from proprietary businesses, professions, or consulting.

  • Freelancing & consulting income
  • Partnership firm share of profit
File ITR-3

Corporate & Entity Filings

ITR-4 (Sugam)

Presumptive Business

For individuals, HUFs, and partnership firms with presumptive business or professional income up to ₹50 Lakhs (Section 44AD/44ADA).

  • Presumptive business (44AD)
  • Professional presumptive (44ADA)
File ITR-4
ITR-5

Partnerships & LLPs

For firms, Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs), Association of Persons (AOPs), and Body of Individuals (BOIs).

  • Registered LLP return filing
  • Partnership deed declarations
File ITR-5
ITR-6

Corporate Companies

For public and private corporations registered under the Companies Act, excluding entities claiming Section 11 exemptions.

  • Private & Public Limited filings
  • Balance sheet & audit compliance
File ITR-6
ITR-7

Trusts & Institutions

For charitable/religious trusts, political parties, research associations, and institutions claiming exemptions under Section 11.

  • Charitable & religious trusts
  • Exemption compliance reporting
File ITR-7
